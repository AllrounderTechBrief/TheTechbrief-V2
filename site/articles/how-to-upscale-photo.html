How to Upscale a Small Photo Without Making It Look Worse

By The Tech Brief Editorial Team · Published October 1, 2026

Upscaling enlarges a photo by adding pixels. AI upscalers estimate fine detail, which often looks sharper than simple stretching, but they cannot recover detail that was never captured.

Set expectations

Modest enlargements (about 2x) usually look best. Very small or heavily compressed images may show artificial textures or smoothed faces after upscaling.

Steps

  1. Start from the original file, not a screenshot or a re-saved copy.
  2. Choose a modest scale first and compare it to the original at 100% zoom.
  3. Check faces, text and fine patterns for odd artefacts.
  4. Export, then optimize the file size before putting it on a website.
