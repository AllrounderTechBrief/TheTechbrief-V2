# YouTube SEO & Growth Plan — "Shri Ram Rameti Rameti" (Ram Naam Jaap ×108)
This plan covers only the Ram Rameti video. No one can promise 10M views. The plan aims for a video that earns watch time for years (devotional jaap videos grow through search and "play on loop" use), plus a channel that compounds. Keyword ideas below are hypotheses; validate them in YouTube Studio → Research and search autosuggest.

## 1. Video facts (what the title may honestly say)
- 62:23 long, 1080p. Opens with a 24-second cinematic story: Shiva tells Parvati the meaning of the Ram name. Ram Rameti jaap is audible from the first second and rises to full volume at 0:19.
- The jaap loops 108 times without a break (each round of your 39-second track = 1 round). If a round is not exactly one full 2-line shloka, say "108 Rounds" instead of "108 Times" in the title.
- Shloka (as sung): श्री राम रामेति रामेति रमे रामे मनोरमे । सहस्रनाम तत् तुल्यं रामनाम वरानने ॥
  The widely printed form is "श्री राम राम रामेति…". Use the sung form on screen, and put both spellings in the description and tags so both searches find you.

## 2. Titles (≤58 visible characters, keyword first)

## 7. Retention (the part that moves views)
- First 24 s: story hook plus the audible shloka. Target 50%+ viewers still watching at 0:30.
- Long jaap: scene changes every 20–30 s (already built in), no black screens, audio loop has no gap.
- Chapters, a pinned comment ("Comment Jai Shri Ram 🙏"), end-screen link to the next video. Reply to comments in the first hour.
- Shorts (15–30 s, 3–5 per week): cut the story hook ("Shiv ne Parvati ko Ram naam ka rahasya bataya"), the shloka reveal, and a 108 counter teaser. Each links to the full video in the pinned comment.

## 8. Format ladder
3 min hero video → this 108-round 1-hour video → 3-hour and 8-hour sleep versions (keep the story intro, change the visuals) → daily Shorts.

## 9. Timing
Publish 5–7 AM IST. Verify dates on a panchang: Sharad Navratri starts about 11 Oct 2026, Dussehra about 20 Oct, Diwali about 8 Nov. Publish 1–2 weeks before each.

## 10. Targets (to judge, not to promise)
- Click-through rate 5–8% in the first 48 hours (below 4% → change thumbnail/title).
- Average view duration: 40%+ on the 3-minute version, 15–25% on the 1-hour version, watch time as the main goal.
- Subscribers per 1,000 views and returning viewers show the channel compounding.

## 11. Risks
1. Audio rights: your voice is yours; the Suno music needs a paid plan with commercial rights.
2. Repetitious content: a looped hour can be flagged if every upload is identical; vary the visuals and keep the story intro and meaning text.
3. AI disclosure: tick the "altered or synthetic content" box in Studio if asked.
4. Accuracy: keep the title, thumbnail and description spelled the same as the audio; do not claim "108 times" unless it holds.
5. No bought views or engagement.
