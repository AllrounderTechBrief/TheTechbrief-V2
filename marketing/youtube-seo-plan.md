## C. Thumbnails
- Big text, 2 words max: `हनुमान चालीसा` or `श्री राम`. Text must be readable at 120 px wide.
- Face or deity in the left half, large, eyes visible. Gold on dark, with a vignette to stand out in a white feed.
- One small badge: `5 MIN`, `1 HOUR`, `FULL`. Never a badge that the video does not deliver.
- Keep one consistent template per series (gold lettering, same layout) so the channel is recognizable.
- Test two variants (with and without badge) for 48 hours; keep the one with higher CTR.

## D. Description template
First 150 characters decide the click and the snippet:
```
श्री हनुमान चालीसा – full path with a powerful, peaceful rhythm. Listen every Tuesday and Saturday for strength, courage and protection. 🙏

(then lyrics, meaning, chapters, links)
```
Include in the body:
1. Hook line (keyword + benefit) in line 1.
2. Meaning in Hindi and English (2 lines each).
3. Chapters that match the real timestamps.
4. Full lyrics (searchable text, already prepared).
5. Playlist and next-video link.
6. 3 hashtags only, for example `#HanumanChalisa #JaiBajrangbali #Bhakti` (Ram video: `#RamNaam #ShriRam #Mantra`).
7. AI disclosure line: "Artwork is AI-generated devotional art."

## K. Targets (use to judge, not to promise)
- Impressions CTR: 5–8 % in the first 48 hours (below 4 % → change thumbnail/title).
- Average view duration: 40 %+ on 3–10 min videos; 20–30 % on 1-hour+ loops.
- Returning viewers and subscribers per 1,000 views are what compound the channel.
- Expect the first 3 months to be slow; double down on whichever format earns the most watch time.

## L. Risks
1. **Audio rights:** your voice is yours; Suno music needs a paid plan for commercial use; check before monetizing.
2. **AI disclosure:** label AI-generated or altered visuals in YouTube Studio when asked.
3. **Repetitive content:** do not upload identical loops with only the title changed; each upload needs unique visuals or meaning.
4. **Respect:** keep traditional depictions; no sensational text.
5. **Do not buy views or engagement.** It gets channels terminated.
